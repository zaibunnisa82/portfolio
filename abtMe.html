Research Profile

About Me

I am a PhD researcher specializing in Artificial Intelligence for Healthcare, biomedical signal processing, deep learning, explainable AI, federated learning, and digital twin technologies. My research focuses on developing intelligent, trustworthy, and clinically meaningful AI systems that can support diagnosis, decision-making, and personalized healthcare. I have more than 8 years of research experience. I am also working as an independent researcher at "New Uzbekistan University." My research work is mainly around using AI specifically machine learning and deep learning, for healthcare issues. I have a strong grip on the interdisciplinary topics that involve deep learning and neurological issues. As a part of PhD research work, I have developed a model that can use dual data modalities for the detection of neurological disorders. Strong background in interdisciplinary research, reproducible machine learning, and academic teaching, with motivation to contribute to diverse research environments emphasizing scientific excellence, mobility, and societal impact.
